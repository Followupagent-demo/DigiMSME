Real numbers before the vendor calls start

An estimated 32 lakh weddings were expected across India in a single wedding-season window (November 4 – December 14), generating around ₹3.75 lakh crore in business — a volume of simultaneous demand compressed into a few weeks.

— Deccan Herald, citing the Confederation of All India Traders (CAIT) ↗

India's average wedding budget stood at ₹39.5 lakh in 2025, up 8% year-on-year — ranging from ₹15-25 lakh for a Tier-2 city wedding, to ₹35-38 lakh in a metro, up to ₹58 lakh for a destination wedding.

— WedMeGood Annual Wedding Industry Report 2025-26 ↗

Those ranges are exactly why a couple asking "what's this actually going to cost?" needs a real number fast, not a vague estimate a week later — by the time a planner gets back to them with a rough figure, they've often already gotten a faster answer from someone else. A guest count, per-plate cost and venue budget plugged in here gets them a realistic total in the same conversation, while the quote is still theirs to win.
