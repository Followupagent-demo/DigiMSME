Project in Noida Not Converting Enquiries Into Site Visits? Here's Why

Plenty of enquiries come in through listing portals and ads. Very few of them turn into an actual site visit — usually because the follow-up that would convert them arrives too late, if it arrives at all.

A Noida project running ads or listed on property portals often generates a healthy volume of enquiries — and a much smaller number of actual site visits, because the gap between someone showing interest and someone getting a confirmed visit slot is where most of that interest quietly evaporates. A buyer who enquired on Tuesday and gets a callback on Friday has usually moved on by then.

Why enquiry volume doesn't automatically mean sales volume

Sales teams juggling enquiries from multiple channels — portal leads, walk-ins, referrals — often don't have a single system tracking which ones got followed up and which ones went cold, so the same leads slip through every campaign cycle without anyone noticing the pattern.
