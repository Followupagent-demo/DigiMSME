Real Estate Project in Pune Selling Slower Than Expected? Check the Follow-Up Gap First

Pune's homebuyers increasingly compare projects online before ever visiting in person — and the developer who replies fastest to that first enquiry usually gets the first site visit.

A Pune project with genuine value — good pricing, good connectivity — can still underperform on sales velocity if the enquiry-to-visit process has friction. Buyers researching digitally before committing to a site visit typically shortlist based on which developer actually engaged them quickly and clearly, not just which listing looked best.

Why digital-first buyers punish slow follow-up harder

A buyer comparing projects primarily online, rather than by driving to each site, has an even lower tolerance for a slow reply — there's no physical momentum from having already visited, so a delayed follow-up loses them almost entirely to whichever project engaged first.
