Is Your College's Admission Number Dropping in Delhi? Here's a Reason Worth Checking

Good faculty, decent placements — and admission numbers sliding anyway, often because enquiries from prospective students and parents aren't getting a fast enough answer to keep them from choosing elsewhere.

A student comparing colleges for undergraduate or professional courses in Delhi typically has five or six admission-office phone numbers saved, and messages most of them the same week asking about cutoffs, fees, and hostel availability. The college that replies fastest with clear, specific answers tends to stay on the shortlist. The ones that take days to respond, or send a generic brochure link, quietly get crossed off.

Why admission numbers can drop even when the college itself hasn't changed

Admission cells are usually staffed for processing paperwork, not for real-time enquiry handling during the exact weeks when prospective students are comparing options across multiple institutions at once. A promising enquiry that doesn't get a same-day answer often just becomes an enrolment at a college that answered faster — the same lead-response gap that shows up at coaching institutes and schools shows up here too.

What actually fixes this

An instant WhatsApp reply to every admission enquiry with real, specific information and a way to book a campus visit or counselling call, plus automatic follow-up for anyone who enquired but hasn't confirmed yet.
