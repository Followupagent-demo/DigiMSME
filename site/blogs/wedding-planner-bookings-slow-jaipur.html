Wedding Planner in Jaipur Losing High-Value Bookings? Check Your Response Time First

A serious enquiry with a real date and a real budget lands in the DMs — and sits there, buried under comments, until the couple books someone who replied first.

Jaipur is one of India's biggest destination-wedding markets, which means a wedding planner here is routinely competing against other planners for the same high-value couples, often over Instagram DMs and WhatsApp rather than phone calls. A serious enquiry — real date, real guest count, real budget — can sit unnoticed in a DM inbox next to a hundred comment notifications until the couple has already booked someone else.

Why a strong portfolio isn't enough on its own

With this much competition for destination weddings specifically, couples researching planners typically message several at once and go with whoever makes the process feel easiest from the very first reply. A planner with genuinely beautiful past work can still lose the booking purely because the enquiry got lost in a crowded inbox.
