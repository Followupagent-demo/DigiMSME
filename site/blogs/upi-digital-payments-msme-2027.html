UPI Is Set to Process ₹356 Lakh Crore by 2027-28 — What That Means for the Shop Still Taking Only Cash

UPI already accounts for 83% of India's digital payment volume. A business not set up for it isn't just behind — it's invisible to a generation of customers.

For a growing share of Indian customers, especially younger ones, "do you take UPI" isn't a courtesy question anymore — it's a filter. A business that only takes cash isn't seen as old-fashioned. It's simply skipped.

UPI's transaction value is projected to reach ₹356.3 trillion by 2027-28, already accounting for 83% of India's total digital payment volume after growing at an 89.3% five-year CAGR.

— PwC, The Indian Payments Handbook 2025-2030 ↗

Accepting UPI was step one. Reconciling it is the part most businesses skip.

Most MSMEs already accept UPI — that battle is largely won. What's still mostly manual is matching each payment back to the right invoice or customer, a task that eats a genuine chunk of a bookkeeper's week once transaction volume grows past a few dozen a day.

What's actually worth automating next

Not payment acceptance — reconciliation. Matching incoming UPI credits to invoices automatically, and only flagging the genuinely ambiguous ones, is the unglamorous next step that actually saves hours as UPI volume keeps climbing toward 2027-28.
