India's Construction Materials Market Is Set to Cross $80 Billion by 2028 — Contractors Are Choosing Whoever Answers First

A contractor needs samples and a quote this week for a site decision. By the time the sample arrives three days later, the order's already placed elsewhere.

A contractor on a live site needs to decide this week — tile samples, a real quote, a decision the client is waiting on. A three-day gap between asking and getting an answer isn't a minor delay in this business; it's long enough that the order goes to a showroom that answered faster.

India's construction materials market is projected to reach $82.5 billion by 2028, growing at a 10.6% CAGR, as infrastructure spend and residential construction both accelerate.

Sample-and-quote speed is the whole competition

Contractors comparing showrooms aren't usually comparing price alone — they're comparing whoever can actually move at the pace a live site demands. A browsable catalog that lets a contractor shortlist before even calling, and a same-day quote instead of a multi-day one, is what actually wins that decision.

What this looks like in practice

Sample requests routed straight to WhatsApp instead of a register nobody follows up on, and quote templates that make same-day pricing the norm. As the materials market keeps growing through 2028, response speed — not just inventory — is what decides who gets the order.
