98% of Customers Search Online Before Visiting a Local Business. Is Yours Even Listed?

Local search has become the default first step before any local purchase. A business without a real, active listing is skipped before it ever gets a chance.

Before calling, before walking in, most customers now search first — for the business itself, for reviews, for whether it's actually open. A business with no listing, or an outdated one, doesn't get a chance to make its case. It just doesn't show up.

98% of consumers now search online to find local businesses, up from 90% in 2019 — and businesses with an optimized Google Business Profile are 70% more likely to be visited, while about three in four local searches happen on mobile.

— Local SEO industry research, 2026 ↗

"We're already on Google" and "we show up when it matters" are different claims

Having a listing at all is table stakes now, not an advantage. What actually moves a customer from search to visit is a profile that's active — real photos, recent posts, a way to message directly — versus one that's technically there but hasn't been touched in months.

What keeps a listing actually working

A consistent posting cadence, not a one-time setup — because a stale profile signals the same thing to a search algorithm and a browsing customer: this business isn't actively here. As local search keeps becoming the default first step, staying visibly active is what separates a listing that converts from one that's just present.
