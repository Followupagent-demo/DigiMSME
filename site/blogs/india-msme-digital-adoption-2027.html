73% of Indian MSMEs Say Digital Tools Already Grew Their Business. Here's What the Other 27% Are Missing

Most MSMEs have solved the easy layer of going digital — accepting a UPI payment. The harder, more consequential layers remain almost as manual as a decade ago.

Ask most MSME owners if they've "gone digital" and the honest answer is yes — they accept UPI, maybe run a WhatsApp Business number. Ask if a customer inquiry gets answered within the hour, every time, without someone remembering to check, and the answer is usually no.

Over 73% of small businesses across semi-urban and rural India report increased income or improved operational efficiency from adopting digital tools — yet only 13% actively use digital marketing or e-commerce to reach customers, even as over 90% already accept digital payments.

— SIDBI 2025 MSME Digital Survey, via YourStory ↗

The gap isn't willingness. It's which layer got digitized.

MSMEs digitized the easiest, most visible layer first — taking a payment — while the harder, more consequential layers (being found, following up fast, converting an inquiry into a sale) remain nearly as manual as a decade ago. That's exactly the gap this data is describing: real growth already proven from digital tools, concentrated in the one layer that was easiest to adopt.

What the growth actually rewards next

Not a bigger digital overhaul — the specific, narrow layer most businesses skipped: instant reply, reliable follow-up, and being findable when a customer actually searches. The businesses in that 73% already proved digital tools work. The opportunity through 2027 is extending that same proof past payments.
