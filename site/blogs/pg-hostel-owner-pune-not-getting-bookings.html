Own a PG in Pune? Here's Why Students Book Elsewhere Before You Even Call Back

Near FC Road, Viman Nagar, or Hinjewadi, a student's PG search is over within an hour of starting it — often before an owner busy with move-in day even sees the message.

Pune's student and IT-professional PG market moves fast — a new admit or new joinee typically messages a handful of PGs the same afternoon and books whichever one confirms a room and a move-in date first. An owner busy handling that week's move-ins, or dealing with a maintenance issue, often doesn't see the new enquiry until the evening, by which point the seeker has already paid a token elsewhere.

Why this keeps happening every admission and hiring season

The volume of enquiries in Pune spikes hard around college admission season and IT hiring cycles, exactly the weeks an owner has the least free time to reply personally. There's no real system tracking who asked, what they asked for, or whether anyone followed up — so genuinely interested enquiries quietly go cold.

What actually fixes this

An instant reply the moment someone messages or calls — confirming availability, price, and a way to lock a visit — means the PG stops losing bookings purely to response speed.
