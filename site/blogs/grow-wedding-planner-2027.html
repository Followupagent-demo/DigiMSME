India's Wedding Industry Is Headed Past $228 Billion by 2030 — Most Planners Still Lose the Booking in a DM

A high-value inquiry lands in the DMs — a real date, a real budget — and gets buried under a hundred comments before anyone replies.

Someone messages about a date six months out, ready to talk budget. It's a genuinely high-value inquiry — and it sits in a DM inbox next to a hundred comment notifications, easy to miss entirely until the couple has already booked someone who replied.

India's wedding services market was valued at $105.55 billion in 2024 and is projected to reach $238.18 billion by 2030, growing at a 14.53% CAGR — fuelled by rising spend on destination weddings and personalized experiences.

— TechSci Research ↗

The market is growing fast. Most of that growth is going to whoever replies first.

With roughly 8-10 million weddings a year and rising per-event spend, the volume of inquiries most planners get isn't the problem — losing the high-value ones to a slow reply is. A portfolio that shows real work, and every inquiry routed to WhatsApp instead of a social DM inbox, is the difference between catching that inquiry and never knowing it existed.

Heading into the next wedding season

Nothing about the actual planning work needs to change. What needs to change is making sure a genuine, high-value inquiry never gets buried again — because by 2027, the planners capturing this growth will be the ones who never let that happen in the first place.
