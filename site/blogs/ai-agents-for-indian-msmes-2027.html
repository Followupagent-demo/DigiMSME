87% of Indian Enterprises Are Already Using AI. Small Businesses Are Catching Up Faster Than Anyone Expected

AI in Indian business isn't an enterprise-only story anymore. SMBs are adopting it at a faster year-over-year rate than large companies.

"AI for business" still sounds, to a lot of MSME owners, like something built for companies with an IT department. That gap is closing faster than most people running a small business have noticed.

87% of Indian enterprises are actively using AI solutions, per NASSCOM's AI Adoption Index — and small and mid-market businesses are now reporting higher year-over-year AI adoption growth than large enterprises.

— NASSCOM AI Adoption Index ↗

The real shift isn't chatbots — it's narrow, specific automation

The AI actually driving this SMB growth isn't a general-purpose assistant — it's narrow, specific automations: a lead scored automatically, a review replied to, a quote drafted in seconds instead of thirty minutes. That's a materially lower bar than "build an AI strategy," and it's exactly why smaller businesses are catching up fast.

What this actually means for a business without an IT team

Nobody needs to hire a data science team to benefit from this shift. Automating one specific, painful task — the one that's currently costing the most time or the most lost customers — is the entire on-ramp, and it's the same on-ramp enterprises used before them.
