What renovation actually costs per sq ft right now

Full home renovation costs in India typically run ₹800-₹3,000 per sq ft depending on scope and material grade, with luxury renovations exceeding ₹5,000/sq ft — and overall renovation costs have risen roughly 12%-18% since 2024 on higher material and labour prices.

— AECORD / NoBroker home renovation cost guides, 2026 ↗

This calculator's default rate of ₹1,800/sq ft sits comfortably in the mid-segment band that most metro renovations actually land in — basic cosmetic work (repaint, basic flooring, fixture swaps) starts lower, around ₹500-800/sq ft, while premium finishes and structural changes push well past ₹3,000/sq ft. The point of a range instead of a single number is that the final cost genuinely depends on material grade and site condition — which is exactly why this estimate is a planning-stage starting point, not a quote.
