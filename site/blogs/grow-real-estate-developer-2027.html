India's Real Estate Market Is Racing to $1 Trillion by 2030 — Is Your Project Even Findable?

Three in four homebuyers now research digitally before ever calling a developer. A project with no real digital presence is invisible to most of its own buyers.

A prospective buyer finds a project listing, fills in an inquiry form out of genuine interest — and waits. By the time anyone calls back, they've already shortlisted two other developers who replied same-day.

India's real estate market is projected to grow from $350 billion in 2023 to $1 trillion by 2030, with 75% of homebuyers now relying on digital platforms and half doing virtual property tours before ever visiting in person.

— Construction Week India, citing Aurum PropTech ↗

A 3-day follow-up gap isn't a small delay anymore — it's most of the buyer's decision window

When three-quarters of buyers are already comparing projects digitally before they ever pick up the phone, a slow follow-up doesn't just annoy one lead — it hands the decision to whichever developer's microsite and WhatsApp line responded while the buyer was still actively comparing.

What a project needs heading into 2027, not eventually

A project microsite that captures intent the moment someone lands, instant WhatsApp follow-up instead of a multi-day gap, and a one-tap site-visit booking — not a redesigned brochure, a faster first response. The market is growing regardless; which developers capture that growth increasingly comes down to who answers first.
