India's K-12 Market Is Headed Past $100 Billion — Enrollment Season Is Won or Lost in the First Missed Call

A parent calls during admission week. The line's busy. They message instead — and get a reply six hours later, by which point they've already called another school.

Admission week means every phone line is jammed and every inbox is behind. A parent trying to enrol their child doesn't wait patiently — they call the next school on their list, and by the time anyone follows up on the first message, the decision's already made elsewhere.

India's K-12 education market is projected to grow substantially through 2030, driven by NEP 2020 implementation, a 260-million-strong student population, and rising private-school enrollment as household incomes grow.

— TechSci Research ↗

The growth is real. Most of it still gets lost to a busy phone line

Private schooling is expanding fastest exactly where parents have real choice between multiple schools — which means the schools capturing new enrollment aren't necessarily the ones with the better facilities, just the ones that answer first during the two-week window that actually matters.

What closes the gap before the next admission cycle

An enquiry form that never routes to a busy line, instant WhatsApp auto-response with next steps, and a Google Business Profile parents can actually find mid-search. Small, specific fixes — aimed at the exact two weeks a year where most of a school's growth is decided.
