Automobile Showroom in Pune Seeing Low Footfall Despite Strong Enquiries Online?

The listing gets viewed, the enquiry gets submitted — and the test drive never gets booked, because the follow-up that would close that gap arrives too late or not at all.

A Pune showroom running listings and ads often sees healthy online interest — views, enquiry-form submissions — that doesn't translate into actual footfall, because the gap between an online enquiry and a booked test drive is where most of that interest quietly disappears.

Why online interest doesn't automatically become showroom visits

A buyer researching a vehicle online is usually comparing two or three showrooms at once, and the one that responds fastest with a confirmed test-drive slot tends to get the visit — regardless of which showroom actually has the better deal.
