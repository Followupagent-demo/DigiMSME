What homes actually cost, and how fast that's moving

Average residential prices across India's top 7 cities rose about 8% in 2025, from roughly ₹8,590/sq ft at end-2024 to ₹9,260/sq ft at end-2025 — with NCR the standout, posting double-digit growth while most other metros saw single-digit appreciation.

— ANAROCK Research ↗

Over a 5-year window, ANAROCK data shows construction costs across India's top 7 cities up roughly 34%, while average housing prices in those same cities rose about 59% — appreciation that has outpaced the cost of actually building the homes.

— ANAROCK Research, via Business Standard ↗

That appreciation is exactly why the loan amount a buyer types into this calculator keeps climbing every year, even for the same 2BHK — and why a same-day EMI number, backed by a real bank rate rather than a guessed one, is what actually moves a hesitant lead toward booking a site visit.
