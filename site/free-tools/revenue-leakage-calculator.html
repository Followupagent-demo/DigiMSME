What's actually walking out the door to quick-commerce

Nearly 80% of consumers have shifted at least a quarter of their grocery spending to quick-commerce apps, and over 2 lakh kirana stores closed in a single year — yet local stores still hold 88% of India's retail food market on trust, credit, and zero delivery fees alone.

— Outlook Business, citing AICPDF ↗

Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart together control over 90% of India's quick-commerce order volume, with the sector's gross merchandise value estimated at USD 6.1 billion in 2024 and projected to grow at a roughly 48% CAGR through 2028.

— Datum Intelligence, cited via Startupfeed ↗

That second number is the one that matters for this calculator: a market growing at nearly 50% a year isn't a passing phase a kirana store can wait out — it's the size of the tap this tool is measuring. Every "can't visit today" enquiry that goes unanswered doesn't just lose one order; it's one more data point nudging that customer's default habit toward whichever app answered in nine minutes.
