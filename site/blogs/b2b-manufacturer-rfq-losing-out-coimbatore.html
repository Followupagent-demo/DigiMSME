B2B Manufacturer in Coimbatore Losing RFQs to Slower But Faster-Responding Competitors?

A buyer sends the same enquiry to three factories at once. The order usually goes to whoever replied first with a real quote — rarely the one with the better product.

A Coimbatore manufacturer with genuinely strong product quality can still lose RFQs it should have won, simply because buyer enquiries sit unread for a day or more while the shop floor handles production and stock counts. By the time someone gets to it, the buyer has already ordered from a competitor who replied the same day.

Why product quality doesn't guarantee the order

Buyers sending the same RFQ to multiple suppliers rarely wait to find out which one is actually better once someone else has already replied with specs and pricing — response speed becomes the deciding factor long before product comparison ever happens.

Every inquiry pushed to WhatsApp instead of a shared inbox nobody checks fast enough, and quote templates that make same-day response the default. Run AsliKaam's free Check Your Business tool to see this built around your own factory, or WhatsApp us directly.
