Review Request + AI-Drafted Reply 4 min read

Consumers Trust Reviews Less Than They Used To. Here's What Still Moves the Needle.

Trust in reviews has fallen from 84% at its 2016-17 peak to 42% today. What's replacing blind trust in the star rating is scrutiny of how a business actually responds.

For years, the advice to local businesses was simple: get more reviews, keep the star rating high. That advice is getting less useful every year — not because reviews stopped mattering, but because fewer people are taking them at face value.

Consumer trust in online reviews as much as personal recommendations from friends and family has fallen sharply — from 84% at its 2016-17 peak to 42% in BrightLocal's 2025 Local Consumer Review Survey.

— BrightLocal, Local Consumer Review Survey 2025 ↗

If the rating alone doesn't build trust anymore, what does?

As blanket trust in star ratings erodes, the review thread itself becomes the signal a prospective customer actually reads: not just what happened, but whether the business responded, and how. An unanswered review — good or bad — reads as an unmanaged business. A thoughtful reply, even to a middling 3-star review, reads as one that's actually paying attention to what customers say.

Why "ask for a review" and "reply to every review" have to be the same system

Most businesses only engage with reviews reactively — after a bad one forces a scramble. Asking for a review right after good service, while the experience is still fresh, produces the positive volume. Replying quickly and consistently to whatever actually comes in, good or bad, is what a visitor notices when comparing a few similar-looking businesses in a search result. Doing only one half of that is most of why review sections end up looking abandoned.

An AI-drafted reply, reviewed and approved by a human before it posts, doesn't replace judgment on how to handle a genuinely difficult review. What it removes is the far more common failure: "I'll get to it later" turning into never, simply because writing a reply from a blank screen takes longer than it should for something this repetitive.
