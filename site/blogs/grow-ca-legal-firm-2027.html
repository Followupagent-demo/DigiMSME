72% of Indian Law Firms Have Already Gone Digital. Has Yours?

A prospective client searches "CA near me." Three other firms show up. This one doesn't — not because the work is worse, but because it was never set up to be found.

A prospective client searching for a CA or a lawyer nearby isn't researching credentials first — they're just seeing who shows up. A firm doing genuinely good work for existing clients can still be invisible to every new one searching that exact phrase, simply because nobody ever set up a proper listing.

Approximately 72% of Indian legal firms have already adopted technology-enabled solutions to enhance service delivery and client engagement, as the sector's digitization accelerates alongside more than 100,000 active CA firms nationwide.

— Grand View Research / Mordor Intelligence ↗

"Findable" and "good" are two completely separate problems

Most CA and legal firms solved trust years ago — reputation, referrals, repeat clients. What most haven't solved is showing up for the client who doesn't have a referral yet and is just searching. That's a findability gap, not a credibility one, and it's the one costing new clients every single week.

What closes it

A credibility-first site that actually shows expertise and track record, plus WhatsApp consult booking so a search result turns into a scheduled meeting instead of a closed tab. With over two-thirds of the industry already moving this direction, standing still is itself a competitive decision.
