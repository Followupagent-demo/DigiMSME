Wholesale Distributor in Surat Losing Retailer Accounts to Competitors?

A retailer calls to reorder, gets hold music and a hand-read price list, and calls a different distributor instead — not because of price, but because reordering was friction.

A Surat wholesale distributor with genuinely competitive pricing can still lose retailer accounts if the actual process of placing a repeat order is slow — a hold queue, someone reading out a price list by hand, a callback that takes hours. A retailer in a hurry to restock simply calls a different distributor instead.

Why retailers leave over friction, not price

A distributor doesn't need to win a retailer's trust from scratch — that relationship already exists. What's actually at risk is losing it anyway, purely because reordering from a competitor's live catalog takes thirty seconds and reordering by phone takes ten minutes.
