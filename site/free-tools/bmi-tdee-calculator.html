Why this calculator's "Normal" range isn't the same for every body

BMI classification — WHO global vs. ICMR/WHO Asia-Pacific cutoffs for Indian bodies
CategoryWHO global cutoffICMR (2022) / WHO Asia-Pacific cutoff
Underweight< 18.5< 18.5
Normal18.5 – 24.918.5 – 22.9
Overweight25.0 – 29.923.0 – 24.9
Obese≥ 30.0≥ 25.0

Source: Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), 2022 guidelines, and WHO Asia-Pacific BMI classification. Cutoffs vary slightly across published summaries — treat as a directional guide, not a clinical diagnosis.

Indians and other South Asians tend to carry more visceral fat — the fat around internal organs that drives metabolic risk — at a lower body weight than Western populations, which is why ICMR and WHO's own Asia-Pacific guidance recommend lower cutoffs: overweight from a BMI of 23, not 25. A trial member who reads "Normal" on a Western BMI chart may already be in the overweight range by the standard actually relevant to their own health risk — a distinction worth surfacing before, not after, they sign up.
