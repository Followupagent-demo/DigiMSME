Own a PG in Bangalore? Here's Why Tech-Park Enquiries Never Turn Into Bookings

A young professional near Koramangala or Whitefield messages three PGs before their commute even starts. The one that replies before their next meeting gets the deposit.

Someone relocating for a new job near HSR Layout, Koramangala or Whitefield doesn't have time to visit six PGs in person — they shortlist by WhatsApp first, comparing whoever answers with a real price and photos against whoever leaves them on read. In a city where a new hire's first weekend is often the only time free to actually shift, the PG that replies within the hour is the one that gets picked, regardless of which room is actually nicer.

Why Bangalore PGs lose bookings they should have won

Enquiries in Bangalore tend to spike in short, sharp bursts — new joinee season, lease-renewal season — and most PG owners are running the property solo or with one caretaker, not a booking desk. A burst of five WhatsApp messages in one evening often means two get answered and three get forgotten, and there's rarely a way to tell which ones those were.

What actually fixes this

The fix isn't hiring a receptionist — it's making sure no enquiry, missed call, or unanswered WhatsApp message just goes quiet. An instant auto-reply the moment someone reaches out, with real availability and a clear next step, closes the exact gap costing bookings today.
